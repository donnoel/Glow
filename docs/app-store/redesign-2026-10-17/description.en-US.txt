Glow Daily Practice is a calm place to build habits, track your water, and see how your routines take shape over time.

Make room for the small things that matter: a morning walk, a few pages of a book, time to stretch, or a daily journal. Choose your days, set a reminder, and check in with a tap.

YOUR DAY, CLEARLY IN VIEW
See what is due, what you have completed, and what is coming up. Correct a check-in with a quick undo and keep going at your own pace.

WATER, ALONGSIDE YOUR HABITS
Log a drink with a quick amount or enter your own. Adjust your daily goal, choose fluid ounces or milliliters, and edit previous entries. Review 7- and 30-day reports against each day's saved goal, with days you did not log clearly marked.

FIND YOUR WEEKLY RHYTHM
See scheduled habits and completed check-ins together. Weekly summaries and highlights help you understand your patterns. Open any habit for streaks, weekly completion, and a monthly history.

GLOW ON APPLE WATCH
Check off today's existing habits, undo a check-in, and log 8, 21, or 24 US fluid ounces of water from your wrist. See daily water progress and use Undo last drink to make a correction. Watch actions sync through your paired iPhone.

MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
Use a focused daily view on iPhone or the native sidebar and wide Insights layout on iPad. Add a Home Screen widget for a glance at today's habit progress. Keep active and archived practices organized in your Library.

Small actions add up. Glow helps you notice them, one day at a time.
